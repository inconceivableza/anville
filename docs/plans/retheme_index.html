For mentors

Whatever You Do is a pathway for discerning your calling, drawing on scripture, a strengths assessment, feedback from trusted peers, and a mentor to help you unpack your gifting.

A calling workbook for every season of life

How it works

Three simple steps, taken at your own pace, to help you see how God has shaped you and where that might lead.

Use this with my organisation
01

Take the assessment

  • Sort 36 statements about yourself
  • From “Definitely not me” to “Real strength”
  • About ten minutes, online
02

Five friends do the same

  • Invite five people who know you well
  • They answer the same questions about you, anonymously
  • See their answers beside your own
03

Process it with a mentor

  • A pastor, colleague or wise friend
  • Two or three conversations to make sense of it all
  • We send them a short briefing pack

How the assessment works

The difference it makes

We ask participants the same questions on the first and last day of the course.

71%

had a more detailed understanding of their God-given gifts, talents and limitations

86%

had a more detailed understanding of what the Bible teaches about work and calling

100%

had a clearer practical plan for how to pursue God’s purpose for their life
