Everyone has a purpose – do you know yours?

Whatever You Do is a pathway for discerning your purpose.

  • Scripture
  • A strengths assessment
  • Feedback from trusted peers
  • A coach to help you unpack your gifting

How it works

Five simple steps, taken at your own pace – with a coach alongside you throughout.

The difference it makes

71%

had a more detailed understanding of their God-given gifts, talents and limitations

86%

had a more detailed understanding of what the Bible teaches about work and calling

100%

had a clearer practical plan for how to pursue God’s purpose for their life

For churches

Releasing and equipping your members for kingdom impact – in church and at work.

Most church members spend 95% of their week outside the church building. Whatever You Do equips them to see that everyday work as ministry.

  • Run it togetherTake your congregation, small group or staff team through it as a course.
  • Raise up coachesEquip wise people in your church to walk alongside others, with a short briefing pack.
  • Free for participantsNo cost to the people you send through it.
