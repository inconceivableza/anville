# An expired link still withdraws what its holder gave

Withdrawing must stay as easy as giving was (Article 7(3)). An observer's own link, and an accepting coach's, stopped withdrawing the day they expired, which left someone who could take their answers back on Monday to ask the people who run the service on Tuesday. They now keep working for that one purpose: an observer who sent answers can withdraw them through their own link after it expires, and a coach who accepted can withdraw their acceptance (ADR 0024). Answering and accepting still stop at expiry. This decision was first written as amendments in ADRs 0005 and 0024, and is kept here instead.

> ✨ Drafted with AI assistance.

## Considered options

**Leave withdrawing to the controller once the link has expired, as ADRs 0005 and 0024 first had it.** Rejected: a link lapses on a schedule (30 days by default) that has nothing to do with when someone changes their mind, and the link is the one way to take back what they gave without writing to anyone.

**Let the participant's copy of an observer's link withdraw too.** Rejected: the participant holds a copy of every link and it is no secret, so it never withdraws, expired or not. Once an observer claims their own link the copy stops working (ADR 0009).

**Name nobody on the expired page, in case an old link had been passed on.** This was the first version, replaced the same day (29 Oct): the link named the participant while it worked, so naming them tells whoever holds it nothing new, and the page now gives its own address too, since that is what to keep.

## Consequences

- The page says the link has expired and thanks the person, then, quietly below, offers to withdraw what they shared, naming the participant and giving its own address to come back to, and says that this can't be undone. "Withdraw my data" reads as a link in that sentence but is a button that posts, since mail filters open every link in a message.
- An own link with nothing sent is refused as any dead link is, having nothing to take back, as is an expired coach's link that declined or never answered. A person who has lost their link, or whose link was revoked or reissued, still asks the controller (ADR 0007).
- The observers' notice, the coach's line above "Accept →" and the privacy page say that the link also withdraws after it has expired.
- The participant is told how many people withdrew their answers, as a count on their response, and never which, since they never see who answered either: below the list on Five trusted voices and beside the card's Answered count. A withdrawn observer's row looks as any other's. A retake starts the count again at none (ADR 0025).
