# Group results combine self-results only, never per person, from three members

An organisation admin or group admin may see a group's results combined: the self-results of its members, averaged, with every number hidden while fewer than three members have a self-result. Nobody but the participant, and their coach on the participant's consent (ADR 0011), sees one participant's result. Observer results are left out because the observers' privacy notice says only that the participant may show what they see to a trusted third party; combining them for a group's admins would need that notice changed first.

The minimum of three matches the observer minimum. It does not make a member's result impossible to work out: in a group of three, an admin told two members' results can deduce the third's. That is accepted, as it is for the observer average.

Consent covers this: the consent text says that if you join a group, its admins see your progress and the group's combined results, and the page for joining a group names the organisation and the group.

## Considered options

**A minimum of five members.** Rejected: small cohorts are common, and three is the minimum already accepted for observers.

**Combine observer results as well.** Rejected for now: it needs the observers' notice changed, and their answers would then reach people the participant did not choose.
