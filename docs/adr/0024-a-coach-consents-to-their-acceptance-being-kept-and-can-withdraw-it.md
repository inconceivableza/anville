# A coach consents to their acceptance being kept, and can withdraw it

A kept "accepted" implies the coach's Christian faith (ADR 0010), and real testers are about to arrive, so its lawful basis is settled as the coach's explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), given by accepting on their link. Consent must be as easy to withdraw as to give (Article 7(3)), so the coach's link, which until now offered nothing once accepted, offers a way to withdraw that answer, as an observer's link already does for theirs.

## Considered options

**The exemption for a not-for-profit religious body's own members and regular contacts (Article 9(2)(d)).** Rejected: it holds only if the controller is such a body, which is not yet settled, and only for its members or regular contacts, while a participant may name anyone as coach. It would also leave the coach no way out of their own.

**Keep nothing until the legal list is settled, with testers naming a pretend coach.** Rejected: testers are meant to walk the coach's part for real, and the fix is small.

## Consequences

- The line above "Accept →" reads as the consent it is: what is kept, who is told, and that the coach can withdraw it on the same link.
- Withdrawing deletes the acceptance and stops the link working, so the coach no longer sees anything of the participant's (ADR 0011). The participant's coach card then says the coach has withdrawn and offers choosing someone else, as after a decline. A section already opened by the coach's acceptance stays open (an opening condition, once met, is not taken back: ADR 0019).
- A coach whose link has stopped working (expired) cannot withdraw in the app and must ask the controller, the same open question as an observer's (spec, Legalities are parked, not forgotten).
