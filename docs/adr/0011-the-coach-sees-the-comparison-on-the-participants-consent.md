# The coach sees the comparison on the participant's consent, kept on the coach's link

Once the coach has accepted and the participant has visited their comparison, the participant may tick "Give my coach access to my results and this comparison", and the coach's link then shows the results and the comparison as the participant sees them (ticket 27). The comparison is built from observers' answers, so a third person now sees what observers said about the participant: the observers' means, how far they agree, and the distribution strip of each observer's percent, lowest first. Someone who knows the observers could read more into the strip than the participant can. The coach sees nothing below the observer minimum, through the same suppression as the participant, and the observers' privacy notice says the participant may also show what they see to a trusted third party. It names no number: a participant who changes coach and ticks again has shown it to two people, and revoking cannot take back what the first one saw.

The consent is kept on the coach's link, beside the coach's answer, not on the participant's response. Revoking or reissuing the link, saving another coach or removing this one then ends it with nothing extra to clear, and a new coach never inherits it. The tick is offered on the comparison alone, since the coach sees both pages and the comparison is the later of the two.

## Considered options

**Show the coach the means but not the strip.** Rejected: the coach would read a different comparison from the participant's, and still see observers' answers combined, so the notice would need the same sentence.

**Keep the consent on the participant's response.** Rejected: every way of ending the coach's link would have to clear it too, and one forgotten would hand the next coach the results unasked.

**Leave the observers' notice as it was.** Rejected: it said only the participant sees their answers combined, which would understate who does.

## Consequences

- An observer who answered before the notice changed was told less than is now true. There is no real data yet; once there is, a change to who sees observers' answers needs them told.
- What the coach sees changes as the participant's comparison does: a new observer's answer reaches the coach's link too, while the consent stands.
- One consent covers the results and comparison of the pathway's one sort. A pathway with a second scored block would need the consent to name which.
