# A coach's acceptance is kept, though it implies their faith

The coach's link asks them to accept or decline six commitments, and only the answer is kept, on the link, never which boxes were ticked (spec, Coach; ticket 13c). Accepting needs every box, and one box is "I'm walking with Jesus myself", so a kept "accepted", beside the coach's name and email address, still records that this person affirmed a Christian faith: special category data about the coach (Article 9), whatever else is left out. A decline says nothing about which commitment was the reason. We keep the answer anyway, since ticket 27 gives the results only to a coach who accepted, and the coach's page says before they answer what is kept and that the participant is told which.

**Keep nothing, and let the coach tell the participant themselves.** Rejected: ticket 27 needs to know the coach accepted before showing them anything of the participant's.
