# A retake deletes what observers sent, and keeps what was finished

A participant can retake the strengths evaluation from their results page, on any system, where until now only a demo system could (fix-19). Retaking clears their sort, its result and its tie-break. It also deletes every observer assessment of them, after a warning on the page that asks first, and opens answer collection again (ADR 0017). The people they invited keep their links, so each can send again, and is reminded as before they sent (ADR 0016). The coach no longer sees the results until the participant chooses to show the new ones (ADR 0011).

The sections the participant finished stay finished, and their visit to the comparison and their view of it stay, so nothing already opened locks again, such as Section 2's first workbook, which waits on the comparison (ADR 0019).

> ✨ Drafted with AI assistance.

## Considered options

**Keep what observers sent.** Rejected: the comparison would set a new self-assessment beside answers sent while the old one stood, and the participant is told the answers go before they choose.

**Refuse a retake once anyone has a link.** Rejected: the participant could then never sort again once they had invited people, which is when a first attempt is most likely to be regretted.

**Take back each finished section whose gate needs the sort, as the demo retake did.** Rejected: it locks again what the participant has already opened and worked through, Section 2's workbook among it, for a choice that is only about the sort.

## Consequences

- Deleting is final: nothing of the old answers is kept to restore, and the count of observers who withdrew starts again at none, since nobody did.
- A participant who retakes needs enough people to answer again before the comparison shows, while the sections they finished stay finished.
- The coach page says the participant is yet to finish the evaluation until they choose to show the new results.
