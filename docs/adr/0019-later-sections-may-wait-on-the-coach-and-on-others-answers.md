# Later sections may wait on the coach and on others' answers

Until now a participant was never held back waiting on someone else: Section 2 needed only that a coach and three people had been sent links, not that anyone had answered (ticket 40), and Section 1 stopped requiring the comparison to have been visited (ticket 52, reversing 16c). After the 20 Oct demo the content owner asked for the opposite in two places, and we agreed both for *Whatever You Do* (the faithful port keeps the original prototype's order).

- **Section 3 opens only once the coach has accepted.** A section may carry opening conditions, clauses from the gate's fixed set that must hold before it first opens (ADR 0003), and Section 3's is that the coach has accepted. An acceptance on the coach's current link counts even after that link expires, as the coach card already shows it; revoking or reissuing the link, or choosing another coach, clears it. A decline means choosing someone else. Once Section 3 has opened it stays open, as a hub side card stays complete, so a link revoked later never locks again what it opened.
- **Section 1's reflection waits for the comparison.** The reflection is shown but cannot be written until the participant has pressed "Compare with how others see you →" with at least the minimum number of observer assessments in. Completing Section 1 needs the reflection, so it now waits on three observers. To keep the rest of the pathway moving meanwhile, Section 2 requires the Strengths assessment in place of Section 1, and Section 3 requires both.

> ✨ Drafted with AI assistance.

## Considered options

**Gate completing Section 2 on the coach's acceptance.** Rejected: the condition is about opening Section 3, and a gate would have the participant wait with Section 2's work done but unfinishable.

**Make Walk with a coach complete only on acceptance.** Rejected: Section 2 requires that card too, so Section 2 would wait on the coach as well.

**Count a visit to the comparison below the minimum (16c's rule).** Rejected for the reflection: it would unlock it on the "not enough answers yet" explanation, before any real comparison exists to reflect on.

**Gate completing Section 1 on the visit, leaving the reflection writable.** Rejected: the reflection is meant to be written after seeing how others see you.

## Consequences

- A participant whose people never answer cannot finish Section 1, and so never opens Section 3. A participant whose coach never answers cannot open Section 3. The hub says what each is waiting on, rather than "Nothing is waiting for you right now", and passes over a section whose only open work waits on other people.
- The press that unlocks the reflection is the one that freezes answer collection (ADR 0017), so finishing Section 1 now means closing collection. People invited who have not answered by then are not counted, after the warning that already says so. The reflection stays unlocked if withdrawals later unfreeze collection.
- The participant holds a copy of the coach's link and could accept in the coach's place (spec, Coach), so the rule is only as strong as that.
