It asks what the visitor wants, at least one of three (ticket 111, ADR 0027), each choice's value one of SignUpChoice.Choice's, and the thanks says what happens next for each one ticked. "Join the waitlist" and the footer's "Contact us" lead here, naming their own choice for the server to tick (`stay` in the query), and the address takes the focus, by `autofocus`, only in a form a link opened that way (stay.focus_email): a plain visit does not take the focus, and a refusal leaves it on the button pressed (sprint-3d-r1-fix-4). The links carry no fragment: a browser applies no `autofocus` on a page whose address has a fragment naming an element (the HTML standard's rule), and taking the focus scrolls the address into view, the box around it, by itself (hand check of 17 Nov). Where the form is on the page already, main.js ticks the choice and focuses the address in place; once the thanks has taken the form's place, it asks for this section again by the link's own query (htmx, a GET), which comes back as a fresh form, the address empty. Following the link instead would load the page again, restarting the hero video. A change to what this wording signs a visitor up to raises WORDING_VERSION in engine/stay_in_touch.py, and is checked against the privacy page. novalidate leaves the address to the server, whose refusals show only after the press, beside the button. The trap field is hidden from everyone: a person leaves it empty, and a bot filling every field is thanked with nothing stored. {% endcomment %}
