Every ordinary answer on the page belongs to it by its `form` attribute, each under its own name, and so do the page's presses: its Save (`engine/page_save.html`), its way on and, on the page that seals a letter, the date and "← Previous page". So without JavaScript any press on the page sends every answer on it, which the press stores before anything else, and nothing chosen is lost to a press on something else. A contact list, a coach checklist and a reading's confirmation keep their own forms. It holds nothing itself but the pathway version the page was drawn from, which a first answer is recorded against, and it sits outside `#completion`, which an autosave can redraw out of band, so it is never swapped away. Its address is the Save's, the first of its buttons, which Enter in an answer presses; with JavaScript that Save is hidden and main.js stops the press, so Enter in an answer never reloads the page (fix-7). novalidate leaves the date, as every answer, to the server, whose refusal says why after the press. {% endcomment %}
