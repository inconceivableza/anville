Rendered with the section, and again out of band after an autosave, so the messages and the button follow what the participant has just written rather than waiting for a reload. What it shows depends only on the answers, so a save is enough to bring it up to date. Nothing is offered while the section's activity is shut behind a scripture reading, or while its sort is not yet in. On a page before a section's last, the way on is "Continue →" to the next page, and the gate is that page's own clauses. The wrapper is always here, even with nothing in it, so the autosave always has something to swap into. It is the gate's own state that is refreshed, never the blocks: only a scripture reading opens more of a section, and confirming one reloads the page, so a save can never reveal a block that was not already here. "← Previous page" sits beside the way on, as "Back" sits beside "Continue →" in the coach checklist, and on its own where there is no way on beside it (ticket 41e). The way on is disabled while its gate is unmet, unless the document wants messages only after a press (ticket 83): then it can always be pressed, and a refused press says what is missing. {% endcomment %}
