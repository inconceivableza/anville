{# Copyright (C) New Community Church SE London 2026. #} {# For licensing information see ../../../../LICENSE.md #} {% comment %} ✨ People who know the participant, a first name and an email address per row, as the prototype's contact screen lays them out, each row marked needed or optional as the author numbers them (ticket 51a). Each person's row is a form of its own (`engine/contact_row.html`), which changes them alone, so a page left open can never drop anyone added since, as saving the whole list once did. An empty row adds that person and makes their link in the same act. Below the rows, its authored sentence and "Add another person" (`engine/contact_more.html`) add one more row at a time, rather than one appearing once every row is filled (ticket 70). Every row's fields and its Save or "Get link", and "Add another person", belong to the list's one form here, by their `form` attribute, so without JavaScript each of those presses sends what is typed in every row (fix-1, ADR 0020): the edits to people listed are saved with it, all or none, and the page comes back with what was typed in the empty rows. Only the row whose "Get link" was pressed adds anyone. `press`, a fingerprint of who the list holds, lets a "Get link" sent again by a reload be told from a new one. novalidate, as on each person's form, leaves the email addresses to the server. While answer collection is frozen (ticket 55) the list says why, and has no empty rows; the form stays, for Save. {% endcomment %}
