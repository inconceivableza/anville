How it works

Begin your journey
{# ✨ Ticket 98: the five steps are in the page, so they read without JavaScript; with it, main.js shows them one at a time as tabs. #} {# ✨ Each step carries a drawn screen of the tool in place of a photo, as v3 has them (checklist, 29 Oct), in the tool's own words: decoration, as the step's words say what it does. main.js shows the current one in the panel. #}
  1. STEP 01

    Discover your gifts and talents

    Reflect on your strengths through a guided evaluation and scripture: sort short statements about yourself in about 7 minutes, and explore the gifts described in Romans 12 and 1 Corinthians 12.

    {# ✨ The sort as it deals a card onto its buckets, in the sort page's own classes, so it looks like it (the developer, 29 Oct). #}
  2. STEP 02

    Hear from people who know you

    Invite at least three people you trust to share their perspective, then compare it with your own and notice where your views align or differ.

  3. STEP 03

    Make sense of your story

    Work through guided prompts on your life, your work and the moments that have shaped you, at your own pace, and notice the themes that run through them.

  4. STEP 04

    Explore a sense of calling

    Draw together your gifts, experience and reflections, put your sense of calling into words, and identify practical next steps.

  5. STEP 05

    Return to what you discovered

    Write a letter to your future self, capturing what matters to you now and the steps you want to take, to revisit later.

A coach can walk alongside you throughout. Invite a pastor, colleague or wise friend when you’re ready.

For churches

Releasing and equipping your members to do whatever they do for the glory of God.

Most church members spend 95% of their week outside the church building. Whatever You Do equips them to see that everyday work as ministry.
