Whatever You Do is a pathway for discerning your calling, drawing on scripture, a strengths assessment, feedback from trusted peers, and a mentor to help you unpack your gifting.

A calling workbook for every season of life

How it works

Three simple steps, taken at your own pace, to help you see how God has shaped you and where that might lead.

Begin your journey Use this with my organisation
01

Take the assessment

  • Sort 36 statements about yourself
  • From “Definitely not me” to “Real strength”
  • About ten minutes, online
02

Get feedback from people who know you

  • Five trusted friends or colleagues
  • They answer the same questions about you
  • You compare the two lists
03

Process it with a mentor

  • A pastor, colleague or wise friend
  • Two or three conversations to make sense of it all
  • We send them a short briefing pack
