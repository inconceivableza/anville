About

We believe everyone is designed for a purpose – and that discovering it shouldn't be left to chance.

Whatever You Do is a pathway for discerning your calling – drawing on scripture, a strengths assessment, feedback from people who know you, and the wisdom of a mentor.

We help release individuals and churches to see everyday work as ministry, and to make a kingdom impact wherever God has placed them.

How it works

Four steps, a few hours, and someone walking it with you

The steps below describe the Whatever You Do course. As an organisation we're about vocational discipleship more broadly — but this is the thing you can start today.

The calendar time is longer than the working time, deliberately. You're waiting on people — for your contacts to answer, for your coach to find an hour — and the thinking needs a few nights to settle. Nothing expires, and you can stop wherever you like and pick it up later.

STEP ONE

Find out what other people see

Sort 36 cards to map your gifts, then answer six questions in your own words. Five people who know you well answer the same questions about you.

Most people find the gap between the two lists is the most useful thing they take away.

STEP TWO

Read your own life back

Lay your life out in chapters, then mark what happened in each: the doors that opened, the ones that shut, the people who shaped you, and the work that bore fruit.

Seen together rather than separately, the threads tend to show themselves.

STEP THREE

Put it into words, then open it back up

Narrow everything down to a single sentence you'd actually stand behind. Then deliberately widen again — the same calling could be lived out in far more ways than the two or three you've already thought of.

THROUGHOUT

Someone walking it with you

Not a step so much as the thread running through all four. You choose a coach at the start — someone who'll ask good questions rather than tell you what to do — and we send them what they need before each conversation.

You can start without one, but almost nobody finishes well alone.
