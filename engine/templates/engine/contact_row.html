With JavaScript each of its buttons sends back this row alone, which takes its place, so typing not yet saved in any other row is never lost; without, each leads to the list's page again, opened at this person (fix-2). Every row's fields, and its Save or "Get link", belong to the list's one form (`blocks/contact_list.html`) by their `form` attribute, so without JavaScript each of those presses carries what is typed in every row: edits to people listed are saved with it, and what was typed in the empty rows comes back in them (fix-1, ADR 0020). Each row's `number`, `person` and `drawn` (what its person was when the page was drawn, so only rows changed since count as edits) place what was typed in it; the button names the row pressed. Someone listed is saved as their name or email is typed, only the row's status coming back, the Save button being for browsers without JavaScript. Their own form, holding no fields now, still carries that autosave: it includes their two fields by id. An empty row stores nobody until "Get link", which adds them and makes their link in the same act, so nobody is listed without one. A link just made, or reissued, opens in a dialog to copy, this once (only its hash is kept); without JavaScript the dialog shows open, in the row. Remove, for someone whose link works, asks first: in a dialog here, or on a page of its own without JavaScript. novalidate leaves the email addresses to the server, whose refusal says what is wrong under the fields; without JavaScript, or for a new row, the row comes back with what was typed, the field at fault marked.
