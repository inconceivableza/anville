Privacy notice

Draft wording This notice is a draft for trying Whatever You Do out with made-up answers. The parts marked “To be confirmed” are still being decided. Please do not write anything real about yourself or anyone else yet.

This notice says what Whatever You Do keeps, who sees it, and what you can do about it. It covers the people who take the journey, the people they ask to rate them, their coach, and anyone who leaves an email address on the homepage.

Who we are

The controller, who decides how your information is used and is responsible for it, is To be confirmed: the controller’s name, postal address and contact email address. Ask them anything about this notice, or about what is kept about you.

Registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office: To be confirmed: the registration number, once the data protection fee is paid.

If you take the journey

Whatever You Do is for adults. Everyone taking part confirms they are 18 or over; we do not ask for a date of birth.

What is kept

  • Your account: your first name and email address, your password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it), when you signed up and last signed in, and when you confirmed you are 18 or over.
  • Your consent: which version of the consent text you agreed to and when, and when you withdrew, if you did.
  • Everything you write or choose in the journey, and your results, so you can come back to them. Some of it may be about your faith, your work, your health, or hard times you have been through, so nothing of it is stored until you agree.
  • The names of the people you ask to rate you, with their email addresses, and the name of your coach, if you choose one.

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google handles your sign-in under its own terms. We ask Google only for your email address and basic profile (your name and profile picture), and keep what it sends with your account. Nobody has to use Google: you can sign up with an email address and password instead.

Who sees it

  • You see everything you write.
  • The people you ask to rate you see your first name. They never see your answers.
  • Your coach, if you choose one, sees your first name. If you then choose to share your strengths evaluation results with them, they also see those results and how the people you asked rate you on the same things, until you stop sharing or change coach. They never see the rest of your answers.
  • The people who run this service can reach everything stored, in order to keep it working.

If someone asks you to rate them

The person who asked you gave us your name and email address. We keep them, with your answers and when you opened your link and sent them.

They see nothing of your answers until enough people have answered. Then they see everyone’s answers combined, and each person’s without a name, and they may show what they see to their coach. They never see which answers are yours, but the people who run this service can.

Until you answer, we may email you a reminder once a week while your link works. Every reminder has a link to stop them.

If someone asks you to be their coach

We keep only the name the person who asked you gave us, and your answer, accept or decline. You don’t give us an email address. Accepting means agreeing to every commitment on your link, one of which is about your own faith, so we keep that you accepted only with your agreement, given when you accept. If you decline, they are told only that you declined.

If they choose to share their results with you, your link shows you their strengths evaluation results and how the people they asked rate them, until they stop sharing or choose another coach.

If you leave your email address on the homepage

If you sign up under “Stay in touch”, we keep your email address, when you left it, and which version of the sentence you agreed to, to send you occasional news of Whatever You Do. Only the people who run this service see the list.

What we send by email

  • If you take the journey: messages about your account, such as a link to reset your password when you ask for one.
  • If someone asks you to rate them: the weekly reminders above, until you answer, stop them or your link runs out.

Links for the people you ask to rate you, and for your coach, are never sent by us: you send them yourself.

Who else handles it

This site’s fonts, photos and video are served from the site itself, and it has no analytics or advertising cookies: only the ones it needs to work, such as the one that keeps you signed in. The site is hosted, and its emails sent, by companies acting on our behalf: To be confirmed: the hosting company and the email service, and where they keep the data.

How long it is kept

To be confirmed: how long each kind of information is kept, and what is deleted when. Until that is decided, nothing is deleted after a set time.

How to withdraw

  • If you take the journey: withdraw your consent at any time from “Your consent”, linked at the foot of your hub. That closes the journey until you agree again, and the links you gave your coach and the people you asked to rate you stop working. For now, what you have already written is kept. To have your account and everything kept with it deleted, ask the controller.
  • If someone asked you to rate them: withdraw through your link while it works, and your answers are deleted. After your link has stopped working, ask the controller.
  • If someone asked you to be their coach: press “Withdraw my data” on your link while it works, and your name, your answer and the link are deleted at once. After your link has stopped working, ask the controller.
  • If you left your email address on the homepage: ask the controller, and it is deleted from the list.

You can also ask the controller for a copy of what is kept about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

How to complain

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, please tell the controller first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
