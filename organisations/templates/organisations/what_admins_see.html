{# ✨ What a group's admins see, worded as the consent text's group line (access/consent.html). The join page and "Your groups" include it unchanged, so the promise reads the same wherever it applies. #}

Its admins, and its organisation's admins, see your first name, how far through you are and when you were last active, and the self-assessment results of the whole group averaged, once at least three members have them, never yours on their own. They never see your answers. If you leave the group, they no longer see anything about you through it.
