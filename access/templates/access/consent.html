Before you begin

Draft wording This text is a draft for trying the app out with made-up answers. Please do not write anything real about yourself yet.

What is stored

Everything you write or choose in the pathway is saved to your account, so you can come back to it. Some of it may be personal: your faith, your work, your health, or hard times you have been through. Because it can be this sensitive, nothing is stored until you agree.

Who sees it

You do. Nothing is shared with a coach or anyone else unless you choose to share it. The people who run this service can reach stored answers in order to keep it working.

Your choice

Agreeing is up to you, and your enrolment code does not agree for you. If you do not agree, nothing you write is stored, and you can agree later. You can withdraw your consent at any time from this page.
