Before you begin

Draft wording This text is a draft for trying Anville out with made-up answers. Please do not write anything real about yourself yet.

What is stored

Everything you write or choose in the pathway is saved to your account, so you can come back to it. Some of it may be personal: your faith, your work, your health, or hard times you have been through. Because it can be this sensitive, nothing is stored until you agree.

Who sees what

You see everything you write.

People you ask to rate you see your first name. They never see your answers.

A coach, if you ask someone to be yours, sees your first name. If you then choose to share your self-assessment results with them, they also see those results and how the people you asked rate you on the same things, until you stop sharing or change coach. They never see the rest of your answers.

A group's admins, and its organisation's admins, if you choose to join a group, see your first name, how far through you are and when you were last active, and the self-assessment results of the whole group averaged, once at least three members have them, never yours on their own. They never see your answers. If you leave the group, they no longer see anything about you through it.

The people who run this service can reach everything stored, in order to keep it working.

Your choice

Agreeing is up to you, and your enrolment code, if you were given one, does not agree for you. If you do not agree, nothing you write in the pathway is stored, only your account: your first name and email address, your password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it), and when you signed up and last signed in. You can agree later. You can withdraw your consent at any time from this page. If you do, you stay in any groups you have joined, but their admins see only your first name and that you have not consented.
