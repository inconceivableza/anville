Draft wording This text is a draft for trying Anville out with made-up answers. Please do not write anything real about yourself yet.

What is stored

Everything you write or choose in the pathway is saved to your account, so you can come back to it. Some of it may be personal: your faith, your work, your health, or hard times you have been through. Because it can be this sensitive, nothing is stored until you agree.

Who sees what

You see everything you write.

People you ask to rate you see your first name. They never see your answers.

A coach, if you ask someone to be yours, sees your first name. If you then choose to share your strengths evaluation results with them, they also see those results and how the people you asked rate you on the same things, until you stop sharing or change coach. They never see the rest of your answers.

The people who run this service can reach everything stored, in order to keep it working.

Your choice

Agreeing is up to you. If you do not agree, nothing you write in the pathway is stored, only your account: your first name and email address, your password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it), and when you signed up and last signed in. You can agree later. You can withdraw your consent at any time from {{ withdraw_from }}.
