# 25b: A printable copy of Section 1

**What to build:** The participant can print or copy their Section 1 work (their gifts summary and their own results) to hand to their coach themselves. The app sends nothing.

**Blocked by:** 10a (Onboarding completion)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Sprint:** not yet decided; split from 25, carried from sprint 1

**Spec:** User Stories › Coach; Implementation Decisions › Coach; Testing Decisions
