# 62: Before real participants: controller, retention, data protection impact assessment

**What to build:** The three decisions that must be made with the content owner before any real participant's data goes into any instance: who the controller is, how long data is kept, and a data protection impact assessment. Until all three are made, every instance holds fake data only.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-human

**Sprint:** 3 or later; parked until real participants are planned

**Spec:** Legalities are parked, not forgotten

- [ ] A named controller, and a hosting arrangement (who owns the account, who deploys into it) that everyone involved has agreed to
- [ ] A retention rule, with what deletion and export mean when consent is withdrawn
- [ ] A data protection impact assessment, treating the whole reflective corpus as potentially special category data
- [ ] Each decision recorded in an ADR, and the spec's list of parked legal items updated to match
- [ ] No real participant is added to any instance before all three are done

**Context**

- Moved out of ticket 26 on 19 Oct, where it was an open criterion
- Decisions for the content owner, with the developer; not for an agent to make
- UK and EU data protection law both apply. The spec's other parked legal items (processor agreements, the Article 15(4) policy, the privacy notices' wording) stay listed there
