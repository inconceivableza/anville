# 20: Role pictures (card builder)

**What to build:** Section 3c ("Make three of them real"): a card builder where a participant develops two or three role pictures with a title and four fixed sub-fields, with a collapsible worked example, and is gated on having developed enough of them.

**Blocked by:** 19 (Possibilities (idea generator))

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Parent:** whatever-you-do-milestone-1 spec (Demo 3, 9 Oct)

- [ ] The card builder block supports a minimum and maximum number of cards, a title placeholder, fixed sub-fields (who is it for, what would your days look like, what would it take, describe one Tuesday) and a collapsible worked example, all authored in the pathway document
- [ ] Two blank cards are offered at the start; the copy says "aim for three; two is the minimum"
- [ ] The gate requires at least two cards to be developed; the minimum is authored and defaults to two. The prototype counted a single character in any field as "developed"; carry that as the default and record it as weak, so the author can tighten it later
- [ ] Starred possibilities and the calling statement appear pinned above the cards as raw material
- [ ] The block submits one answer that the server validates
