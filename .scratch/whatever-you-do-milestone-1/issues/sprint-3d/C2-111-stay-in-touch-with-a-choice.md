# 111: Stay in touch with a choice

**What to build:** "Join the waitlist" leads to a sign-up box that has no waitlist in it: one email field and a thanks that says "subscribed" (feedback, "Friday 30th - updates", images 46 and 47). The content owner asks for tick boxes saying what the visitor wants: their church on the waitlist, news by email, or to be contacted. Each sign-up keeps what was ticked, so the list can be used for each.

**Spec:** Sprint 3d: the code review and the 30 October feedback; Sprint 3c: the content owner's feedback
