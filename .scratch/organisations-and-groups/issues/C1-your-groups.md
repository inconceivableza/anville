# C1: Your groups

**What to build:** A participant follows "Your groups" from the foot of their hub, sees each group they are in and what its admins see, and can leave any of them.

**Blocked by:** A1 (Organisations, groups, membership and permissions, with can_see)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec:** Pages; User Stories › Your groups

- [ ] "Your groups" sits at the foot of the hub beside "Your consent" and lists each group's name and organisation, with a sentence saying what its admins see
- [ ] Admins' names are not shown
- [ ] "Leave this group" asks for confirmation, then ends the membership; the participant's answers are untouched
- [ ] After leaving, the group's admins can no longer see the participant's progress
- [ ] A participant in no group is told so
