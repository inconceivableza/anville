# B1: Consent v2

**What to build:** The consent text says what a group's admins see if the participant joins a group, and everyone who agreed to the earlier text is asked again.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec:** Consent v2 (ADR 0004); User Stories › Consent

- [ ] The consent text adds that if you join a group, its admins see your name, how far through you are and when you were last active, and the group's results combined, never yours on their own, and that leaving the group ends that
- [ ] The consent text's version is raised, so a participant who agreed to the earlier version is asked again before reaching the pathway
- [ ] The text stays a marked draft
